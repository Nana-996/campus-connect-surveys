# Inclusive product copy

## Goal
Present CampusVerify as a survey and research platform for students, professional researchers, and community respondents without changing pages or instructions that intentionally apply to one account type.

## Changes
- Rewrite the homepage headline, supporting text, proof points, FAQs, footer, and sharing metadata so all user types are represented.
- Review shared public pages and shared help/support wording for claims that CampusVerify is student-only, and replace only the inaccurate general product descriptions.
- Preserve student-specific rules and pages, including academic-email verification, school partnerships, student pricing/credits, campus targeting, and student-only workflows.
- Preserve General/Researcher-specific pages and rules.
- Check the updated pages on mobile and desktop and confirm the app has no new errors.

## Technical details
- Copy-only frontend changes; no account logic, eligibility, pricing, targeting, or database behavior will change.
- Route metadata will be updated alongside visible page copy so search and social previews use the inclusive positioning.
